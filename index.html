About & My Shop

A Boutique Built on Detail

Highly adaptable and tech-savvy, I combine an Information Technology background with real, hands-on entrepreneurial experience running a premium online vintage boutique — handling everything from brand positioning to the final "delivered" notification.

I manage the end-to-end operations of Blush & Bows, a premium vintage and coquette clothing e-commerce store, scaling a devoted customer base into international markets across the US and Europe.
